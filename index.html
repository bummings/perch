Bioactive Terrarium Workshop

Tuesday 9/15 @ 6:30PM

Taught by Rae from Detritus & Decay Art (@detritusanddecayart). Build your own miniature tropical ecosystem with a clean-up crew of isopods & springtails.

In this workshop you will learn:

  • Techniques of soil layering
  • Terrarium plant choice (2–3 plants provided)
  • Watering methods
  • Best placement for your vessel
  • Plant care & tips
  • Springtail & isopod care info

Use a provided glass container or bring your own closed glass container (around 1 gallon, with a lid, and can fit your hand inside).

Feel free to bring your own decor items such as crystals, stones, or figurines!
